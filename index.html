Household Waste & Recycling

#0012025—2026

I like data. I also like recycling. So, predictably, I started weighing everything I dispose of at home. Feel free to explore the data below.

Monthly totals can be viewed by material or combined. Imputed figures spread each material type’s weight across the days since the previous collection, giving a more realistic picture of when it accumulated. Recorded shows the weights on the dates they were collected. The year-on-year comparison uses equivalent periods in 2025 and 2026. Where recent residual waste has not yet been collected, an explicitly provisional estimate is shown. Data tables and methodology explanations are provided for each chart.

Recycling rate is the share of all recorded weight collected for recycling, including food recycling, soft plastics and batteries. General waste counts towards the total but not the recycled amount. Figures use collection dates, with no estimates. Rolling periods are measured back from today; calendar years may be incomplete.

Each square represents a calendar day. Darker squares indicate more weight recorded for the selected material; pale squares have no recorded weight. Weights are assigned to the date logged, not spread across the days when waste accumulated. The colour scale adjusts to the selected material.
