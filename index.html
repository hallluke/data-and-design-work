I like data. I also like recycling. So, predictably, I started weighing everything I dispose of at home. Feel free to explore the data below.

Monthly totals can be viewed by material or combined. Imputed figures spread each material type’s weight across the days since the previous collection, giving a more realistic picture of when it accumulated. Recorded shows the weights on the dates they were collected. The year-on-year comparison uses equivalent periods in 2025 and 2026. Where recent residual waste has not yet been collected, an explicitly provisional estimate is shown. Data tables and methodology explanations are provided for each chart.
